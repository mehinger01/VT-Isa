Session Agendas

Session 2: Simplify, reset, and move forward.

A 60-minute working session focused on consolidating systems, protecting energy, and making completion decisions.

5 min — Check-in and session goal

Identify what feels manageable, what feels stressful, and what would make the session useful.

15 min — Map the current system

Review each calendar and task tool for purpose, overlap, and actual value.

10 min — Choose a source of truth

Select one primary planning view, one primary task list, and a simple checking routine.

15 min — Build an after-school reset

Create a repeatable transition that restores energy and includes a restart cue.

10 min — Introduce “Done Is a Decision”

Practice choosing completion, strong, or showcase effort.

5 min — Choose the week’s experiment

Pick one strategy to test and define how success will be measured.

Extension

Begin an energy map

Identify high-, medium-, and low-energy times during a typical day and match tasks to the energy they require.
