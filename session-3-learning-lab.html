Step 2 · Before class

Prepare

You are not trying to teach yourself the whole lesson. You are giving your brain a head start.

Discriminant: Δ = b² − 4ac

The discriminant comes from the quadratic formula and predicts the type of roots a quadratic has.

Pretest: For x² − 5x + 6 = 0, calculate Δ.

Step 3 · During class

Capture the structure

Do not copy every word. Record only the rule, why it works, and one example.

Step 4 · After class

Retrieve, then fix

Keep your notes closed until you finish the first three questions.

1. State the three discriminant cases from memory.

2. For x² + 6x + 9 = 0, calculate Δ and describe the roots and graph.

3. For 2x² + 4x + 7 = 0, calculate Δ and describe the roots and graph.

Now check and repair

Reference: Δ > 0 gives two distinct real roots and two x-intercepts. Δ = 0 gives one repeated real root and one touching x-intercept. Δ < 0 gives two nonreal complex roots and no x-intercepts.
