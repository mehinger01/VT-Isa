Lesson 3 · Starting Efficiently

Turn an assignment into a first action.

The goal is not to plan the entire assignment. The goal is to understand the target well enough to begin useful work within five minutes.

← Back to session agenda

The five-question launch

1

What is the final product?

Name exactly what must be submitted, completed, demonstrated, or brought to class.

2

What controls the grade?

Locate the directions, rubric, required components, length, format, and due date.

3

What materials are needed?

Open only the files, notes, textbook pages, or tools required to begin.

4

What is the first visible action?

Choose an action you can physically see yourself doing: open the document, label the page, write a claim, solve question one, or identify three sources.

5

What can happen in five minutes?

Start a brief launch sprint. Momentum matters more than finishing.

Practice A · Clearly defined

AP Chemistry problem set

Complete problems 1–12, show calculations, and submit a photo before 8:00 p.m.

Practice B · Vague and complex

History research response

Explain how industrialization changed American society. Use evidence from the assigned sources.
