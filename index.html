Session Plans

Session 2: Simplify, reset, and move forward.

60 minutes
5 min

1. Check-In and Session Goal

Review what has felt manageable, what has felt stressful, and what would make today especially useful.

15 min

2. Map the Current System

Walk through each calendar and task-management tool to identify its purpose, overlap, and actual value.

10 min

3. Choose a Source of Truth

Select one primary planning view, one primary task list, and a simple routine for checking both.

15 min

4. Build an After-School Reset

Create a repeatable transition routine that restores energy and prevents a short break from becoming the entire evening.

10 min

5. Introduce “Done Is a Decision”

Practice choosing the right level of effort: completion, strong, or showcase.

5 min

6. Choose the Week's Experiment

Pick one small strategy to test before the next session and decide how success will be measured.

Extension

Begin an Energy Map

Identify high-, medium-, and low-energy times during a typical day and match tasks to the energy they require.
